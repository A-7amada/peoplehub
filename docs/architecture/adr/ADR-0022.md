# ADR-0022: Leave policy defaults, built configurable

Status: Decided 2026-11-27 (owner decision relayed by the main agent from Ahmed, WhatsApp 01:56: write a default leave-policy set and build it configurable). Build status 2026-11-27, unit 2 done (an enabled policy with annual accrual sets the opening entitlement of each new year balance in whole days, rounded down; monthly accrual, carry expiry and decimal units remain for later units); unit 1 done: editable policy and tier table per leave type (Admin > Leave policies), pure rules (tier lookup by service years or age, waiting period, first-year proration rounded down to half a day, annual or monthly accrual), audit of changes, per-employee preview. A policy has an Enabled flag; while it is off the type keeps its fixed days and carry-over, so no balance changes yet. Unit 2 (not built): make Enabled policies drive new balances and carry-over expiry. Unit 3 (not built): half-day and hourly request units, which need balances to move from whole days to decimals. The numbers below are illustrative demo defaults, not legal advice.

## Context
Leave types carry a default number of days and `MaxCarryOver` only (stories US-PH2-001, 002, 003; TRACEABILITY LEV-001, LEV-002, LEV-003). No accrual, eligibility, partial-day units or expiry exist.

## Decision
Build a per-leave-type policy that an administrator can edit, with the defaults below. Nothing is hard-coded to a country. A policy change applies from its effective date and never rewrites past balances.

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accrual basis | Annual grant on the entitlement date (no monthly accrual) | Alternative: monthly accrual (entitlement / 12 per full month). Selectable. |
| Eligibility | Annual leave after 6 months of service | Waiting period in months, per leave type. |
| Entitlement by service | 21 days; 30 days from 10 years of service or from age 50 | Tiers are editable rows (from years, from age, days). |
| First-year proration | Pro rata by full months served | Rounded down to the nearest half day. |
| Units | Days, with half-day and hourly (8 hours = 1 day) per leave type | Hourly is off by default. |
| Rounding | Hours accumulate and round to the nearest quarter hour for display; balances keep exact minutes | |
| Carry-forward | Carry up to the existing `MaxCarryOver`; the rest lapses | |
| Expiry | Carried days expire 3 months after year end | Set 0 for no expiry. |
| Encashment | Not part of this ADR | Settlement formula stays an open owner decision (US-PH2-004). |

## Source note
The 21 and 30 day tiers and the age 50 rule match what Egyptian Labour Law 12 of 2003, article 47 said (https://fahmqanon.com/laws/12-2003/47). That page states the article was repealed from 2025-08-31 and replaced by Labour Law 14 of 2025. The 2025 figures were not checked, so these defaults must be reviewed against the current law before any real use. The specialist review is deferred by the owner, so the demo keeps its "not legally validated" label.

## Consequences
Configurable policy, preview of accrual per employee, audit of policy changes, and tests for proration, tiers, carry-forward and expiry. Each unit ships with a migration for both providers.

## Unit 3 build status (2026-11-28): decimal balances, half-day and hourly requests
- Unit 3a: LeaveBalance (Entitled, CarriedOver, Used), LeaveRequest.Days and LeaveTransaction.Delta are decimals, stored with four decimals (numeric(18,4) on PostgreSQL, REAL on SQLite per ADR-0015). Existing whole-day values convert unchanged. Payroll still takes whole absence days; the demo seed rounds up.
- Unit 3b: a request can be a half day (PartOfDay AM or PM, 0.5 day) or whole hours (Hours 1 to hours-per-day minus 1, Days = hours / policy hours per day, exact to four decimals). Rules: single working day only; allowed only when the type has an ENABLED policy with AllowHalfDay or AllowHourly (otherwise the type stays whole days); half day and hours cannot be combined; the balance must cover the fraction. A morning and an afternoon request on one date can coexist, two of the same half cannot, and part-day requests on one date cannot together exceed one day. Hourly requests carry a quantity only, no clock window, so two hourly requests are checked by total, not by time of day (a known limit). A part-day leave no longer excuses a full-day absence in the attendance view.
- Defaults chosen (config the owner can change in Admin > Leave policies): AllowHalfDay on, AllowHourly off, HoursPerDay 8 (unchanged policy defaults). Illustrative, not legally validated.
- Still not built in this ADR: monthly accrual with proration at the entitlement date, carry-forward expiry at rollover (the policy fields exist and the pure rules exist, but balances do not yet lapse), payroll proration for part days.
